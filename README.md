Rush Hour Kitchen is a fast, cheerful cooking game that runs entirely in the browser from static files. Order tickets pin themselves to the board all day long, each asking for one dish made of three to five ingredients in a fixed order. You build each dish on the plate at the pass: click the ingredient bins on the counter to stack ingredients in the ticket's order, send anything marked with a flame to a pan on the stove first and watch its timer ring so it lands on the plate cooked, not burnt, then serve the finished plate to its ticket before the customer's patience runs out. Every correct serve pays coins and score, a wrong stack can be scrapped with the bin, and the whole lunch rush lasts 120 seconds — when the day timer empties, the till is counted and the day's results go up on the board.

## How to play

- Click **Click to start** (or press Space / Enter) to open the kitchen.
- Read a ticket on the order board: the dish name and its ingredient sequence, left to right.
- Click ingredient bins on the counter to add ingredients to the plate in that exact order.
- Ingredients with a flame badge must cook first: click a free pan to start it, wait for the timer ring to fill, then click the pan again to move it onto the plate.
- When the plate matches a ticket exactly, click the ticket (or press 1–4) to serve it and collect coins.
- Wrong ingredient? Click the red scrap bin to clear the plate and start over.
- Serve as many dishes as you can before the 120-second day timer runs out, then click **Cook another day** to play again.
